## What NOT to Include (for Gong positioning)

- Campaign performance metrics as the headline achievement (e.g., "drove 40% increase in email open rates") — these signal campaign operator, not systems architect. Move them to supporting details, not headlines.
- "Marketing Operations Manager" as the primary identity label — "Marketing Systems Architect" or "Marketing Technology Architect" reads better for this role
- Long lists of tools without context — for Gong, each tool entry should have a brief "what you did with it" note, not just a logo
- Generic AI statements like "comfortable with AI tools" — replace with specific deployments
